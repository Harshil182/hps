A Letter From My Heart

Words that have lived quietly inside my soul, waiting for you.

To the love of my life, always

My Dearest Harshi,

If anyone had told me before I met you that a single human smile could illuminate the darkest days, I might never have fully believed them. But then you walked into my world, effortless and radiant, and suddenly everything before you felt like a prelude to the true beginning of my life.

There is a tender poetry in the way you exist, Harshita. The thoughtful tilt of your head when you listen, the soft warmth that spills over whenever you laugh wholeheartedly, and the quiet resilience you carry within you. You bring peace into rooms that were once chaotic, and warmth into corners I didn't realize were cold.

"Loving you is not something I do; it has become the very melody of who I am."

I cherish the grand moments, but I adore the quiet, unscripted ones even more: our sleepy morning murmurs, our silly inside jokes that make zero sense to anyone else, the sweet safety of resting my head near yours, and that unmistakable feeling of being completely and undeniably home.

Whatever storms may wander through our future, I want you to remember that you will never have to face them alone. My hand will always reach for yours; my arms will always be your sanctuary; and my love will remain your steadfast anchor through every sunrise and midnight yet to come.

Eternally, unconditionally, and proudly,

Yours Always
